## Direction contract

THESIS: Cash read the way a T-Sprint team decides. The page refuses the category default of KPI tiles over a generic line chart; instead a plain verdict sentence leads, figures sit in an exact margin beside every chart, and coloured sticker dots are the single state language (solid = actual, ring = forecast, colour = severity).

OWN-WORLD: Flat forest-green shell field (#173A2D) owns the header and tabs; whiteboard-white work area (cool, faint green tint, not cream); sticker dots in green/orange/coral/yellow/blue for state only; Thinking Spree orange marks forecast. Serif voice (Source Serif 4, italic second line) only for the verdict; Schibsted Grotesk with tabular figures for everything else. Hairline rules, white watch-list notes on the wall, no shadows beyond one soft step. Dark mode: the wall turns forest-black, the dots keep meaning.

STORY: The founder sees the verdict and cash in one line, sees why (in/out, who paid, what's due), tries a what-if, and trusts it because Books balance and every figure shows its working.
